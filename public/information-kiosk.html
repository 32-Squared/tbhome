Turtleboards

An NFT collection minted on the Enjin Matrixchain.

Intended purely as fun art and warm entertainment, all content is created by myself as 32 Squared - the umbrella name for my endeavors.

AI assisted in generating base images and building the software needed for the project. The final images you see and words you read are my own.

Collection Details

The Turtleboards collection is closed with Max Supply of 6000 and Max Token Count of 1.

The collection consists of five seasonal waves of 1000 to be released as follows:

  • October 2026 - Summer Postcards
  • November 2026 - Leafing Autumn
  • January 2027 - Iceboards
  • March 2027 - Spring in Session
  • June 2027 - Celebrate Summer

The remaining 1000 tokens will be used as promotional distribution such as NFT.io Community Beams preceding each wave.

Tokens

All tokens are NFT, Max 1, Collapsing Supply. Every token is unique, and if melted, will never exist again.

All tokens are hidden until purchased or transferred from the minting account. Please see the Reveal Notes for complete details.

All 6000 combinations are finalised. Each Wave will be minted in its entirety before release. After Wave 5, the maximum 6000 tokens will be reached and no further tokens will be minted.

Rarity

NFT rarity rankings are computed by NFT.io Marketplace using the Property quantities present for revealed tokens.

As hidden properties are not computed, rarity values and rankings will fluctuate until the entire collection has been revealed.

When reveal is complete, rarity values will not change unless individual tokens are melted. More details are available in Reveal Notes.
