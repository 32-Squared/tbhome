Collect Turtleboards in about 10 minutes

You'll need a phone, a pen and paper, and a little ENJ.

By the end you'll have your own wallet and know how to buy NFTs on NFT.io.

1

Before you start

Your wallet is yours alone. No account, no email, no sign-up.

That also means nobody can recover it for you, so the next steps matter.

2

Get the Enjin Wallet

Download the free Enjin Wallet app, and only from Google Play or the App Store.

Look for the publisher name Enjin, and ignore lookalike apps.

3

Create your wallet

  1. Open the app and tap Create Wallet.
  2. Choose a password of at least 6 characters.
  3. Tap Generate Backup. You'll see 12 words.
  4. Write them on paper, in order. Then confirm them in the app.
Never share or store your 12 words

Don't save them on your phone or computer, take a screenshot, or give them to anyone. Nobody from Enjin or Turtleboards will ever ask for them. Anyone who has these words has your wallet.

4

Find your Matrixchain address

Your wallet lists four networks: Matrixchain, Relaychain, Ethereum and ETH. Each has its own address.

Tap Matrixchain. Your address is at the top of the screen and starts with ef. This is where your NFTs will live.

5

Get ENJ (the right kind)

You need native ENJ to buy on NFT.io. Here's how the networks fit together:

Matrixchain
Addresses start with ef. Where NFTs live and where NFT.io works. This is the one you need.
Relaychain
Addresses start with en. The network's backbone. ENJ sometimes arrives here first and can be teleported to the Matrixchain.
Ethereum and ETH
Other networks in the same wallet. You don't need them for collecting.
Check before you buy or send
  • Native ENJ only. The old ERC-20 version is no longer sold on exchanges, and not every exchange supports native ENJ. Check that yours does before you buy.
  • Check the address prefix (ef or en) before sending. Sending between a Relaychain and a Matrixchain address can mean permanently losing your funds.
Already have old ERC-20 ENJ?

The Enjin Wallet can convert it to native ENJ. It needs a little ETH for the transaction fee, and the converted ENJ shows up on the Matrixchain after about a day. Read Enjin's migration guide.

6

Connect to NFT.io

  1. Go to nft.io and tap Connect.
  2. Choose Enjin Connect. A QR code appears.
  3. On a computer: scan the QR code with the Enjin Wallet app and tap Approve.
  4. On the same phone: the QR code can't open the wallet directly. Take a screenshot of it, open the wallet's scanner, and choose the gallery/photo option to scan it from the image.
  5. Done when your Enjin Wallet appears in the top right of NFT.io.

Android users can also pick WalletConnect instead.

Only the QR code is safe to screenshot

Screenshotting this connection code is fine. Your 12 recovery words are never okay to screenshot.

7

Buy your first NFT

  1. Browse Collections or NFTs, or go straight to Turtleboards.
  2. Hover over an NFT and tap Buy Now (or Add to Cart).
  3. Tap Confirm Payment, then sign the request in your Enjin Wallet.
  4. Your NFT now appears in your wallet.

You're now a collector!

A Multiverse of NFTs and games awaits. Have fun exploring, or get back to the Boards!

Common mistakes

Sending ENJ to the wrong network (ef vs en), buying ERC-20 ENJ instead of native, storing the 12 words digitally, and downloading a fake wallet.

Some common terms

NFT: a one-of-a-kind digital item you own.

Recovery phrase: the 12 words that restore your wallet.

ENJ: Enjin's coin, used to pay on NFT.io.

Matrixchain and Relaychain: the two Enjin networks.

Beam: Enjin's QR code system for claiming NFTs.
