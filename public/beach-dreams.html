Welcome to Turtleboards!

So why did I build all this? Easy answer: because it's fun. And because it's creative. And because it's warm. That's it.

As with all my projects, and there's been many years' worth, I've achieved my true goal if someone smiles, or has a touch of extra positivity in their day.

Turtles are special to me, I've lived with one most of my life. Slow paced, long lived, carefully assessing their position in the environment. There's a lot of good habits here.

And surfing, this is a dream! (get it, Beach Dreams) I eternally seek sand between my toes, salt in the air, and my next ray of sunshine.

So there we are, the inspiration that shaped the surf. Reimagined and materialised as Turtleboards, I invite you to look around.

It's easy to miss the scenery, so surf slow and enjoy the ride!
