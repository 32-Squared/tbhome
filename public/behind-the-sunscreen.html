Behind The Sunscreen

How do you create a million surfboards? Here's my step-by-step process.

1

Create The Parts

  • The Lineup: 32 Original Turtleboards, some a bit more used than others.
  • Phosphenes: 16 Visualisations, giving “seeing stars” a more entertaining meaning.
  • Island Illusions: 8 Hallucinations, that last wipeout was really hardcore.
  • Edge Ink: 8 Island Designs, keeping the tropical theme strong.
  • Surf Styles: 8 Artistic Representations, a selection of postcard backgrounds.
  • Session Times: 4 Times of Day, all unique and beautiful surfing experiences.
2

Assemble The Boards

Images become layers, these are tagged with Properties and weighted to ensure a clear “common” → “rare” spread of Properties.

From the drafting table, layers now move to a randomiser. Here, every possible combination is determined, then an initial selection of combinations is randomly determined based on weights.

Completing the stage, combinations are manually reviewed and curated into the final pool - 6000 unique combinations for the Turtleboard collection.

3

Turtleboard Factory

The final pool of combinations is then sent to the Turtleboard Factory - well, from the randomiser to the compiler - and Turtleboard production is underway.

Layers are individually compiled for each combination, with all layer properties preserved in the metadata produced along with the images.

The complete collection is now produced, saved and ready for final shipment - umm, I mean Enjin Matrixchain minting!

4

Safety First

No animals were harmed in the process. In fact, they lived a much better life than other employees.

Turtleboards are intended for amusement only and have not been tested in real water.

We all share the ocean, pick up your trash.
