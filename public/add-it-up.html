The Math

Multiply each Property.

Absence of a Property counts for Phosphenes, Edge Ink and Island Illusions, thus “none” is included as a value.

32 × 16 × 8 × 8 × 8 × 4 =

1,048,576 Possible Combinations

3

The Pool

From the 1 million total possibilities, 6000 combinations were randomly drawn.

Weights were applied to Property selection to achieve desired rarities.

The final pool was curated and approved by hand.

4

The Factory

Approved combinations had their layers compiled into a single unique image - literally one in a million and unlike all others.

Layer Properties transfer to the metadata for the exact Properties as seen on the NFT.io Marketplace.

All 6000 NFTs from the collection were then minted together from these images and Properties.

The Pool was divided into four equal Waves plus a reserved quantity for promotion, and the surfing has begun!
